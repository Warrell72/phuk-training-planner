Why the plan looks like this

What informed the provision

The calendar is the output. These are the demand, assurance and delivery inputs used to decide the amount, timing and location of training.

1

Demand

Headcount, known qualification expiries, predicted turnover and capability gaps establish the requirement by course and region.

2

Requirement

Minimum coverage expectations, park facilities and pool staffing models determine which skills must be available locally.

3

Timing

Expiry windows, hiring patterns and seasonal operating peaks shape when training is placed through the year.

4

Location

Site suitability and geographic demand are used to place courses where capacity can support the greatest regional need.

5

Delivery

Trainer availability, course capacity and budget assumptions are then balanced to produce the final provision.
